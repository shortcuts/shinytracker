Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30

App data

shinytracker keeps all your data (caught shinies, checklist state) on your device. None of it is sent to any server. There are no accounts.

Accessibility Service

The app uses an Accessibility Service to take screenshots of your Pokemon GO shiny box and to scroll through it during a scan. This is read on your device only, to match sprites and record catches, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Contact

Questions or requests: report a bug or request a feature on GitHub.
